# Bundled fonts

Latin subsets of two variable fonts, as served by Google Fonts (WOFF2), bundled
so the app starts without waiting on the network and works offline.

| File | Font | Copyright | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open-sans-latin.woff2` | Open Sans | Copyright 2020 The Open Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/opensans) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| `jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2` | JetBrains Mono | Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

The SIL Open Font License 1.1 is at https://openfontlicense.org. It allows the
fonts to be bundled and redistributed with software, provided they are not sold
on their own and keep their copyright notice and license.
